# A reader's large default font: what grows, what doesn't, and how to sweep it

Moved here from MEMORY.md in sprint 71, when it reached its 25 KB load limit. One line each, with
the reason.

- A large default font (CDP `Page.setFontSizes`) grows rem, a media query's rem included, but not the window, so a row that fits at 16 runs off from 21 at 320: let it wrap (flex-wrap, an auto margin to keep an item's side, fixed row heights so nothing moves at 16, a min-width on a slot scripts fill, or the server's empty row breaks), and key what the wrap must move (the bar's scroll padding, `/`'s drawing) on a rem media query or clamp step measured where it binds, with a margin. A word can't wrap, so a display head takes a cap: `min(var(--step), (100vw - 32px) / k)`, k its widest word's em plus about 8%, above the step at 16 px from 320 so nothing moves, the h2's k the h1's times their steps' ratio (`.doc-head h1`, `.sched h2`). Diff boxes on a build, never `next dev`, which served globals.css without new @media blocks (`.crew/sprint/68/`, `.crew/sprint/69/`).
- A slot that holds a client control's place in the server's HTML (the bar's `nav > [data-motion]`, empty until the buttons arrive) can't take its content's width, and a px width doesn't follow the labels' rem: from a 20 px font the group ran out of the slot's left edge and over Read as page (43 px at 1440 x 28). Grow it from the drawn width by the labels' em times its step's gain on the 16 px size, `max(142px, 142px + 5.3 * (var(--step-sm) - 13px))`, or `1.0625rem - 17px` for step-base, whose vw term isn't constant: exactly 0 at 16 px (0 of 14,700 boxes moved), never under it, and the slot is the same empty or full, so the arrival moves nothing (`.crew/sprint/71/`).
- The bar's breakpoints are rem, so at a large font its 48rem and 64rem layouts start at wider windows (at 24 px the bar's control shows from 1152 and its 142 px slot from 1536; 1024 x 24 is the phone layout, the foot's row). Sweep each font at 48 and 64 times it, and one px under each, where a clamp's vw term peaks; Playwright's Chromium, Chrome and the webfont set the three labels up to 7 px apart, so check all three (`.crew/sprint/71/`).
- A row of rem text in a px column wraps rather than overflows: from 64rem the record's column is a fixed 360 px (444 from 80rem), so from a 20 px font its nowrap head row squeezed Rev onto two lines and pushed the nowrap step links past the record, then the window (24 px at 1536 x 24, 66 at 1792 x 28). `flex-wrap` on the row and on the steps, the steps `space-between` with Next `margin-left: auto`, row gaps 0 and 4 from 64rem: a wrap moves only what no longer fits, so 0 of 47,943 boxes moved at 16. Stack the two outward (← Sprint p at the start, Next at the end), never both at the end: there ← Sprint p took the point where the last record's Next had been, so a repeated click on Next went back (Iris's fix round; a check reads `elementFromPoint` at Next's points on record n+1). It follows the numbers' digits, so at 1536 x 24 Next is on Rev's row on /log/1, one row under on 2 to 8 and two from 9, which a riffle through them shows (the hold reads the press's box, so it holds) (`.crew/sprint/72/`).
- A px line-height under rem type stops holding its text at a large font: the yard's hint kept a 14 px line for `--step-sm`, whose text box is 1rem tall (16 at 16, 24 at 24, 27 at 28), so a wrapped hint printed its lines 10 and 13 px over each other at 320 x 24 and 28 and its text ran 1 to 3 px into the hand-off from 20 px. `max(14px, 1.5rem - 10px)` is exactly 14 at 16 and holds the box from 20; a px sum that counted the old line (the ghost caption's 76 px margin) reads it through one custom property, `--hint-line`, so 0 of 141,843 boxes moved at 16 (`.crew/sprint/73/`).
- Text beside a button in a `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` grid keeps the button's whole width at any font, so a large one squeezes the text (and under `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, splits every word: /crew's status was 56 px at 320 x 28). A flex row `row-reverse wrap`, the button `order: -1; flex: none` and the text `flex: 1 1 0; max-width: 100%; overflow-wrap: break-word` puts the text on its own line under the button exactly when its longest word doesn't fit beside it (break-word, unlike anywhere, keeps min-content the longest word, which the line break reads), keeps the button's top and right end, and moved 0 of 14,100 boxes at 16; an empty `::after` with `flex-basis: 100%` and an order between keeps a later item (the entry) off the first line (`.crew/sprint/74/`).
- An auto table's column with a set `width` that may wrap shrinks to its longest word and wraps every cell, room or not ("Lead, AI agent" on two lines at 390 x 20 with `width: 60px`): to let site words wrap only where the table needs it, drop the width too (`width: auto`), keyed on `(width < 20rem)` so 16 px never sees it; `text-wrap: balance` kept "AI agent" whole in Chrome (`.crew/sprint/74/`).
- From 1024 the bar is `lg:h-14` (3.5rem, at least 52 px) on a 1rem top margin, so anything stuck under it at a px `top` goes under it as the font grows: the trials' side at `top: 88px` lost 3 to 24 px of its frame's width dimension from a 26 px font, until it stuck at `calc(max(52px, 3.5rem) + 32px)`. html's `scroll-padding-top: 56px` from 64rem has the same flaw, open as a follow-up (`.crew/sprint/80/`).
