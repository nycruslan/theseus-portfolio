# Large default fonts

- A large default font grows rem-sized chrome but not the window: the wrapped sticky bar's rem rows reached 133 px (23%) of 320 × 568 at 24 px, so size targets in a wrapped or large-font state in px (44), a px measure (`.crew/sprint/68/`).
- A display word that can't wrap is capped at its column over its width in em (`min(step, column / k)`), never hyphenated: the cap doesn't show at 16 px, a hyphen in a title reads as a defect; accept the smaller ratio to the text under it rather than shrink the reader's text, and fit a page's heads together, not one word at a time (the 3-column `.sched` rail is 218 px at 1024; `.crew/sprint/69/`).
- Where step links wrap depending on their text, put each direction at its own edge (back at the start, forward at the end) and click the same point on consecutive records: an end-aligned stack put ← Sprint 10 where Next had been at 1536 × 24 (`.crew/sprint/72/`).
