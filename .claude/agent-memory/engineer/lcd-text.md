# Text over the model's canvas: keeping LCD anti-aliasing through the hand-off

Moved here from MEMORY.md in sprint 45, when it neared its 25 KB load limit.

- Text laid over the model's layer gets a layer of its own when the canvas arrives, and Chromium paints text in a transparent layer without LCD anti-aliasing, so on a runner with LCD text (CI's) every glyph pixel changes at the hand-off (B3.1 counted 8,745 for the particulars). A fill in the film colour keeps LCD text (0 px change), but only when every glyph in that layer lies on an opaque fill: on `/`'s phone row, filling Read as page alone changed nothing (1459 px) while the buttons' labels sat on nothing, and filling both gave 0. `will-change` or `translateZ(0)` also give 0, but only by making the text grayscale for good (`.crew/sprint/39/`, fix round 1; `.crew/sprint/44/`, fix round 1).
- "On the fill" means each glyph's rect inside the fill's rect after snapping, and Chromium rounds a fill's edges to the nearest pixel but snaps a glyph's rect outward. A filled box whose text starts at its own edge (the particulars' terms, `padding: 0`) loses LCD wherever that edge falls half a pixel or more past a whole one: at 1180 × 640 the left edge at 699.984 lost it (8,745 px) and at 700.0 to 700.45 kept it, the fraction alone deciding (a sweep of injected `margin-left`). Layout's 1/64 px units make .984 common. The fix is a fill that reaches past the text: `padding-left: 2px` with the margin less 2 px, so nothing visible moves (1 px is enough in theory; 2 covers glyph ink left of its origin). An edge at a whole pixel (the particulars' right, the viewport less whole margins) is safe. Wa's rule from 1280 still has `padding: 0` and loses it at 1300 × 650; B3.1 asserts the hand-off only at 1440 and 390, so no check sees it (`.crew/sprint/45/`).
- How to see it here: this cloud's fontconfig forces grayscale; `FONTCONFIG_FILE` set to a file that includes `/etc/fonts/fonts.conf` and assigns `rgba` `rgb` (passed in `chromium.launch({ env })`) reproduces CI's text. Screenshot the text's box on the Off path (WebGL refused, no canvas) and on Full once `data-state` is settled, count changed pixels, and count colour-fringed pixels (R, G and B apart by more than 24) in each: LCD text has thousands (6,708 in the particulars), grayscale none. CDP's LayerTree sends no `layerTreeDidChange` in the headless shell, so find what decides a layer by injecting styles into both pages and re-reading (`.crew/sprint/45/`).
