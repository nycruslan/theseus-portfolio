Accepted: light ground #e6f3f1 (oklch 95.5% 0.014 185), ink #0f2220, muted #455e5a, copper accent
#944126 for links only, rule #c5d5d2. Dark: #091413 / #e1eae9 / #96a9a6 / #e79d73 / #283635.
Type: 1.2 → 1.333 scale, base 16 → 18, 2xl off-scale 48 → 76 px. Full spec is in
.studio/sprint/1/direction.md.

Rejected:
- Cream plus terracotta, and logbook plus red lead. That's the default AI look wearing the
  subject's clothes.
- Ground at hue 170, which read mint and clinical.
- Ground at chroma 0.008, which read as timid off-white, close to the template.
- 2xl on the modular scale (40 px at 320), which was too small on phones.

**Why:** the accent means "the new plank", copper that hasn't oxidised yet. The story is the
subject's, and it isn't a category default.
**How to apply:** in later sprints, keep the accent scarce and on links or new parts only. Item 2
(dark theme) should tune the dark hull, not replace it. The owner hasn't reacted yet, so record
any disagreement here.
