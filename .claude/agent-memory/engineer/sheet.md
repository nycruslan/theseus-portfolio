# `/`'s sheet from 1024 px: how the drawing, the particulars, the contact row and the key fit

Moved here from MEMORY.md in sprint 45, when it neared its 25 KB load limit. One line each, with
the reason.

- From 1280 the drawing's width is `--plan-fit` on `main`, `max(--plan-floor, 100svh - --sheet-foot) / 0.33807`, which the particulars' left edge and the short regime read too; the 712 px switch and the contact row's 900 px or 13:8 rule are derived from `--sheet-foot`, so a new foot means sweeping them again, and the contact row's hook is `.title-contact`, whose unlayered `margin-top: 0` beats Tailwind's layered `lg:mt-auto` without `!important` (`.crew/sprint/43/placement.md`).
- From 1024 to 1279 under 960 px tall the same short regime reaches down ("The sheet on a tablet window" in app/globals.css): the drawing is the least of the band, the height's fit and the width that leaves the particulars an 18rem column beside its rightmost line, never under the 240 px floor (at 1024 the cap and the floor meet), and the particulars stand beside it as rows; from 960 tall the sheet is the old one, the particulars a strip row (that rule is now `(height >= 60rem), print and …`, so print keeps the strip). The title block's column is `minmax(27.5rem, 3fr)`, binding only from 1024 to 1058, so the bio keeps four lines (`.crew/sprint/45/placement.md`).
- The crew key shows under the table at every width since sprint 45, on two lines from 1024 to 1247 (`.rev-key > .crew-key-who { display: block }`), as the contact row's two groups are (an `::after` with `flex-basis: 100%` and `order: 1`, the contact links `order: 2`, so Tab keeps the DOM's order). It was hidden from 1024 to 1439 by sprint 40's decision 9 only because the resting links waited at the revisions' foot (B4.22 at 1024 × 768, 9.9 px spare; B4.49 at 1280 × 800, 0.4 px); now the links follow the bio wherever the sheet outgrows the window, so the key can't push them (`.crew/sprint/45/placement.md`, call 2).
- The revision block's room under the table at 1440 × 900 differs by feed state: 74.8 px default and open, 51.3 live (the legend's second line), 45.3 resting (the rest line's 29.5); a 13 px line costs 29.5 with the block's 10 px gap. Measure anything new under the table on the resting fixture first (`.crew/sprint/40/decisions.md`, 8).
- A floor stated as a height but set through a width (`15rem / 0.33807`) lands a layout unit under itself: Chromium floors the width and then the svg's aspect-ratio height to 1/64 px (709.913 → 709.906 → 239.984), which W16's `>= 240` read catches wherever the floor binds. The tablet rule rounds the floor's width up, `--plan-floor-w: round(up, var(--plan-floor) / 0.33807, 1px)` (710 → 240.016), and Lightning CSS ships `round()` unfolded. Wa's rule from 1280 still divides unrounded, unread by any check since no W3 window binds its floor (`.crew/sprint/45/`).
