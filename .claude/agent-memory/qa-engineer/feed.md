# Feed and clock lessons (Probe)

Moved from MEMORY.md at sprint 64. Read before writing a check on /crew's feed, the live layer or anything that polls.

- A feed-backed static page (cacheLife 30 s / 5 min) on a build without the feed serves the build's prerender until it is 30 s old from the build, then one stale answer: poll to the expected state, never assert a first response; give each fixture variant a site of its own (a copied `.next`, since two servers on one `.next` share its route cache on disk), and prove a down variant by showing live first, then switching the feed (.crew/sprint/31/).
- `e2e/crew-feed.ts` is the reusable harness: an https fixture server (openssl per run, NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS on the site, since the Studio link needs https on the feed's origin) and `startSite`, a temp dir with `.next` copied without its cache and with its timestamps (15 MB, 80 ms; fresh mtimes make the prerender look new, a 30 s wait) and every other top-level entry symlinked (`.crew` holds the retros a regeneration reads), spawned detached and killed by process group (.crew/sprint/31/).
- Since the owner's measurement on #78 a failed read (non-200, past 2 s, not JSON) keeps the last good answer while its asOf is within 10 minutes, in module memory: prove a keep by waiting for the fixture feed to count a read past the switch, then 4 s, then reading /crew five times (#78's head read down seven times); a failed-read down variant starts from live at asOf 9 min 50 s (.crew/sprint/31/).
- A feed spec's sites come from `crewTest` in e2e/crew-feed.ts (worker fixture: the feed server, kept sites by name, one variant at a time) and run `mode: "default"`, in order on one worker without serial's skip-after-failure; `/` regenerates apart from `/crew`, so poll `/` itself (`untilHome`, the contract's hooks) after `/crew` shows the state (.crew/sprint/34/).
- A feed spec's waits overlap or blow CI's 25-minute cap: start every site's scenario in a beforeAll with `crew.once` (e2e/crew-feed.ts) and let each check await its own and assert; that took crew.spec.ts from 387 to 86 test-seconds and live-layer.spec.ts from 442 to 201 on 2 workers, its WebGL visits and per-site sweeps run side by side in contexts of their own (sprint 34, fix round).
- A browser poller is driven by `page.clock.install()` then `pauseAt()` before `goto` (React still hydrates: its scheduler uses MessageChannel): after each `runFor`, wait for the poll to reach `page.route` and settle before the next, or polls collapse (20 back-to-back `runFor(30_000)` saw 12), and count a no-clock poll (shown, unpaused) from before the action, since it lands during the evaluate that caused it (e2e/crew-live.ts `livePage`, .crew/sprint/35/).
- On a page on Playwright's paused clock, a wait inside `page.evaluate` (setTimeout, rAF, a smooth scrollIntoView) never ends: wait in Node's time. Since B4.42's 30 s abort, one `runFor` past a poll in flight aborts it before the route's answer lands: step each poll at its schedule (30 s live, 60 s open and resting) (.crew/sprint/36/).
- The live fixture tells 9 valid moments of its 12 (an unknown kind, an unparsable `at`, an empty text): take a fixture's counts from the route's own answer (`toAnswer`), never by re-filtering the fixture, which counted 10 and sent three checks red on a correct build (.crew/sprint/36/).
- axe hangs on a page under Playwright's paused clock (its queue waits on setTimeout): sweep inside `page.clock.resume()` with nothing timed to step (the replay paused or ended), then `pauseAt(now + 50)` (`running` in e2e/crew-replay.spec.ts, sprint 37).
- A spec that needs B4.22's feed states reuses live-layer.spec.ts's kept site names and answers (`home-live`, `{ fixture: "live" }`) under a `once` key of its own: `crew.site` keeps sites by name, so a worker that ran B4b serves them at once, and a fresh one reached all three in 12 to 15 s (.crew/sprint/39/, P3).
- A feed site's name is its fixture channel, matched `[\w-]+` in e2e/crew-feed.ts: "seat-6.1" got 404s and its site read down, "seat-61" ran live (.crew/sprint/41/).
