# A reader's large default font: what grows, what doesn't, and how to sweep it

Moved here from MEMORY.md in sprint 71, when it reached its 25 KB load limit. One line each, with
the reason.

- A large default font (CDP `Page.setFontSizes`) grows rem, a media query's rem included, but not the window, so a row that fits at 16 runs off from 21 at 320: let it wrap (flex-wrap, an auto margin to keep an item's side, fixed row heights so nothing moves at 16, a min-width on a slot scripts fill, or the server's empty row breaks), and key what the wrap must move (the bar's scroll padding, `/`'s drawing) on a rem media query or clamp step measured where it binds, with a margin. A word can't wrap, so a display head takes a cap: `min(var(--step), (100vw - 32px) / k)`, k its widest word's em plus about 8%, above the step at 16 px from 320 so nothing moves, the h2's k the h1's times their steps' ratio (`.doc-head h1`, `.sched h2`). Diff boxes on a build, never `next dev`, which served globals.css without new @media blocks (`.crew/sprint/68/`, `.crew/sprint/69/`).
- A slot that holds a client control's place in the server's HTML (the bar's `nav > [data-motion]`, empty until the buttons arrive) can't take its content's width, and a px width doesn't follow the labels' rem: from a 20 px font the group ran out of the slot's left edge and over Read as page (43 px at 1440 x 28). Grow it from the drawn width by the labels' em times its step's gain on the 16 px size, `max(142px, 142px + 5.3 * (var(--step-sm) - 13px))`, or `1.0625rem - 17px` for step-base, whose vw term isn't constant: exactly 0 at 16 px (0 of 14,700 boxes moved), never under it, and the slot is the same empty or full, so the arrival moves nothing (`.crew/sprint/71/`).
- The bar's breakpoints are rem, so at a large font its 48rem and 64rem layouts start at wider windows (at 24 px the bar's control shows from 1152 and its 142 px slot from 1536; 1024 x 24 is the phone layout, the foot's row). Sweep each font at 48 and 64 times it, and one px under each, where a clamp's vw term peaks; Playwright's Chromium, Chrome and the webfont set the three labels up to 7 px apart, so check all three (`.crew/sprint/71/`).
