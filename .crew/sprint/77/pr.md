## Sprint 77 (S): a Log record opens with its heading on a short screen

**Goal** (from the roadmap's stand-down and sprint 76's follow-up, chosen by Atlas): a visitor who
opens a Log record on a short screen sees the sprint's goal, the record's heading, without
scrolling.

On production today, every one of the 75 records opens with its heading below the fold at 320 ×
568, 360 × 640, 375 × 667 (iPhone SE and 8), 900 × 700 and 1023 × 768. Taller phones, tablets and
desktops hold.

**Size:** S. **Contract:** `.crew/sprint/77/contract.md` (T37).

Building: Probe writes T37, Iris sets the placement, and Forge builds it. Results follow.
