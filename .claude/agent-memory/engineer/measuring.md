- agent-browser 0.39.0's Chrome and Playwright's Chromium set the same text at different widths, because Playwright's Chromium sets glyphs at whole-pixel widths (a line's width comes back as an integer) and agent-browser's Chrome at fractional ones, as real Mac and Windows Chrome do: on `/` at 390 the bio is 5 lines in one and 4 in the other (23 px), and at 1440 the particulars' Reach fits one line in agent-browser but wraps in Playwright (17 px). Measure pinned geometry in Playwright's Chromium, as the checks do (a scratch `chromium.launch()` script importing `@playwright/test` by the worktree's absolute `node_modules` path, since ESM resolves from the script's folder), use agent-browser for looking, and where a fit hangs on a wrap measure it in both: at 1280 the bio's three lines held by 0.86 px in Playwright and took four in Chrome. The two renderers wrap different lines, so a reserve must cover the worst of each: at 390 Chrome sets the bio on 5 lines and Leads on 1, while Playwright sets the bio on 4 and Leads on 2 (below 402). CI installs the headless shell (`--only-shell`; Chromium 156 for Playwright 1.64), and Chrome 153 is `/usr/bin/google-chrome` through `chromium.launch({ executablePath })` (`.crew/sprint/39/`, `.crew/sprint/43/placement.md`, the amendment; `.crew/sprint/44/`).
