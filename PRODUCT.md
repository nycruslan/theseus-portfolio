# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

The owner's confirmed product record (interview, 2026-10-21). It changes only through the owner.

## Platform

web

## Users

- **First: hiring executives and boards** weighing the owner for senior AI-platform leadership
  roles. They decide within seconds and want scope, scale and judgment at once.
- Then engineers and peers, who stay, explore and judge the craft.

## Product Purpose

Ruslan Shulga's portfolio. A visitor should leave believing three things, in this order:
1. he leads AI at scale (the AI platform team behind multi-agent orchestration, hybrid retrieval
   and MCP servers that thousands of employees use);
2. he can run an AI org: the crew of AI agents he designed builds and runs this site in public,
   and that is the proof;
3. he has world-class taste: the craft of the site is the signature.

## Positioning

A portfolio rebuilt in public, sprint by sprint, by an AI crew the owner designed and directs,
where every change is made, reviewed and tested by named agents, under rules the owner set. A
neighbouring site can copy a look; it can't truthfully copy that record.

## Operating Context

- The crew (Atlas, Quill, Iris, Forge, Pixel, Lens, Probe) works in sprints on GitHub; each sprint
  ships one pull request.
- The Studio (a separate app) streams the crew's work live. Visitors only watch: they never start
  or steer the crew.
- `/ask` is the one thing a visitor can spend a model call on. It answers from the owner's facts on
  Claude Haiku, with a Vercel Firewall rate limit and a monthly quota on its AI Gateway key, and
  `/evals` publishes its weekly test scores.
- The crew makes its own calls: Iris sets the design on a Design canvas, and the owner may pick
  another there. The owner answers only what only they can decide, and is otherwise not involved.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **One experience:** the portfolio and the live crew are one site for visitors. The portfolio
  shows the crew at work, from the Studio's data; the code stays in separate parts.
- **Spectacle first:** the first screen is a spectacle, yet the owner's name, role and value are
  readable within a second or two, and a visitor can get straight to the facts.
- **Clean and easy:** easy to navigate and to understand, however much of it is 3D.
- **Unique:** something new for 2027, not an imitation of past award winners.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Theseus, and the idea is the ship of Theseus: a ship rebuilt plank by plank, one
  plank per sprint.

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner's facts: `content/profile.ts` and the case studies in `content/work.ts`. Never invent
  numbers, clients or testimonials.
- The crew's record: `.crew/sprint/*/retro.json` and the Studio's events.
- The research behind the redesign: `design/research/2027-redesign.md`.
- The email is on the site so a hiring executive can reach the owner in one click. The phone number
  and anything about a job search stay off it.

## Product Principles

1. The facts come first, and the spectacle carries them, never hides them.
2. Every visible change of the ship maps to real work by the crew.
3. Show the crew honestly: what it did, what it's doing, and who approved it.
4. Spend novelty on one signature moment; everything else stays familiar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every fact reads without WebGL, JavaScript or motion, reduced motion loses nothing,
and ambient motion can be paused.
