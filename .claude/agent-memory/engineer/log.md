# The Log (/log and /log/n): its layout, its drawings and its steps

Moved here from MEMORY.md in sprint 53, when it neared its 25 KB load limit. One line each, with
the reason.

- The yard (sprint 53): `/log/n` draws the Theseus (`[data-theseus]`, never `[data-plan]` or `[data-ghost]`) right before the ghost, since the 3D chunk reads `[data-model]`'s `previousElementSibling` and the hollowing CSS keys on `:has(+ [data-model])`; a step changes planks, never places (Iris's flip-book rule). From 1024 the header, foot row and record are one column, `.log-side` (`display: contents` below 1024, so flex `order` still reaches each child and the Foot stays `.log-head`'s next element), so neither the drawings nor the record move; rows `auto 1fr` put a taller header's slack under the ghost, where auto rows open a gap between the ships; and the model's layer clips to its grid area, so `.log-ghost + [data-model]` spans both drawings' rows, `1 / 1 / 3 / -1`, or the canvas vanishes (`.crew/sprint/53/placement.md`).
- Below 768 the Foot (Read as page and the motion control) sits under the h1 at the drawings' head, `.log-side > .foot { order: 2; margin: 12px 0 0; padding: 0 }`, as `/` hangs it from the bar: at the header's end, after the count line, it left Full, Calm and Off below a 320 x 568 first screen once the Theseus stood above the ghost (B5.25), and B5.1 wants every `.log-head` line above it. Start again follows it in the DOM, outside `.log-head`, or Tab reaches it before Read as page (`.crew/sprint/53/placement.md`, the addendum; before that, `.crew/sprint/31/`, `.crew/sprint/33/notes-iris.md`, section 5).
- A text-decoration on an inline-flex link reaches every flex item, so an `inline-block` arrow as the link's direct child is still underlined; give the link's words one inner span with the arrow inside it, where an inline-block is an atomic inline the underline skips (the record's step links, `.crew/sprint/53/`).
- A page whose DOM order differs from its visual order (the Log's drawings follow the header and record but CSS `order` sets them above the caption) shifts layout whenever the browser paints HTML cut before the late element; reproduce it with a proxy that sends the HTML in two pieces at a marker (it gave B5.25's 0.06992813107324625 to the digit), and hold the first paint with `<link rel="expect" href="#<next element's id>" blocking="render">`, which React 19 hoists into the head from any component (`.crew/sprint/33/`).
- A block whose rule must span its column while its text keeps a measure: `max-width: none; padding-right: max(0px, 100% - 66ch)` keeps the content box at exactly 66ch, and the percentage resolves against the flex container even through a `display: contents` parent; `.log-head p`'s 66ch (0,1,1) outranks `.log-lines` (0,1,0), hence `.log-head .log-lines` (`.crew/sprint/33/`, Iris's rider on #82).
- Next 16.4 patches `history.replaceState`: data without `__NA` (pass `null`) has Next copy its own entry state in and dispatch a restore, so `usePathname` and its router follow the URL with nothing fetched on an unchanged tree; data carrying `__NA` (`history.state` itself) bypasses the patch, and the router's stale URL is written back on its next commit. The yard's press replaces the URL with `null` (`lib/model/yard.ts`, `.crew/sprint/55/`).
- A group header row (`th[scope=rowgroup]`) inside `.rev`'s folded grid lands in the 36 px number column unless its `tr` and `th` are `display: block`; and a `tbody:first-of-type` rule outranks the fold's own padding, so scope it to the widths it is for (`.crew/sprint/32/notes-iris.md`, Iris's early look).
- A step's words that move an element the page keeps, past Chrome's 500 ms input window, are a layout shift (the question at 23 moved `.record` and `.log-lines`: 0.0253 at 390, 0.0091 at 1440), and a held key or a riffle's stop has no input then; Chromium never counts a newly inserted element, so the yard swaps `.log-head`'s children and replaces `.record` itself (and `.log-revs` when it moves in view), measured 0 in every case. Any new swapped region comes in as new elements, never as a kept element's new children (`lib/model/yard.ts`, `.crew/sprint/56/`).
- A press stepped at `pointerdown` (the yard's mouse press and hold) must let the press's own mousedown focus the link before the words replace it: replaced in a microtask of the pointerdown, focus falls to `main`, and `preventDefault` plus a script `focus()` shows the ring, since nothing had mouse focus before (Iris's review). Swap a task later (a `MessageChannel` message, which Playwright's paused clock doesn't hold), then move focus to the replacement by script: no ring. The click that ends the press does nothing by a flag set at that pointerdown and `event.detail > 0` (a keyboard Enter's click has detail 0); don't clear it on keydown, or Escape mid-hold lets the ending click step (`.crew/sprint/56/`).
- From 20rem to 768 (`screen and (20rem <= width < 48rem)`) a step page's links stand 4 px under the ghost by position, not order: absolutely in main (the DOM keeps them in the record's head, so Tab and Y4 hold), their margin-top `--yard-row` the sum of everything above the ghost's foot, 116 px (five margins, 20 + 12 + 16 + 6 + 14, the control row's 44 and the 4 px gap) plus the h1's and the Theseus caption's lines plus both drawings at 252/632 of `100% - 32px`; the hint's line (`--hint-line` on `.log-open`, 14 px at 16) and the ghost caption's margin (62 px plus that line, 76 at 16) hang off it, and the hint stays one line there at any font, since its width and the block's 20rem floor grow together (`.crew/sprint/73/`). The gap keeps a focused Next's ring (2 px at 2 px) off the ghost's keel, which it crossed by 1.9 to 2.9 px flush. A new margin or line above the ghost must join the sum, or the row floats off the drawing (H1's 1 px sweep, 320 to 767; `.crew/sprint/59/placement.md`, `.crew/sprint/61/`).
- A media query's rem is the reader's default font size, so `20rem <= width` drops a placement that assumes one-line text at a large default font or heavy zoom (24 px moves its floor from 320 to 480), and the fallback needs checking there too: the record's in-flow head overflowed below 20rem until the steps took their own row again (wrap, `flex-basis: 100%`). Set the default font in Playwright with CDP `Page.setFontSizes` (`.crew/sprint/61/`).
- A record's line that shows only in some modes holds its space by `visibility`, never `display: none` (B5.6's C5.2 fails any display-none element in a record, and showing it then moves nothing, CLS 0); the hint `[data-yard-hint]` is keyed by `.log:has(> [data-model][data-yard])` with no script, and a bare `checkVisibility()` calls it shown in Off, so checks pass `visibilityProperty` (`.crew/sprint/59/placement.md`, section 3).
- The chapter line (`chapterLine` in lib/count-line.ts, kept free of `@/` since Probe's H4 imports it) shares the yard's question selector, `[data-yard-question], [data-yard-chapter]`, so it lands with the plank as the question does; the header's `SprintWords` sets both it and the count line with each "sprint n" unbroken (`.crew/sprint/59/`, +11 B gzip on the 3D chunk).
- /log's last element is the door into the trials (`p.log-door` in `section.log-revs`, /log only), whose link joins `.log-start a`'s three selector lists rather than copying them, so the Log's ways out keep one look. Below 1024 `[data-model]` is `position: absolute; inset: 0` in main, so anything added at the foot grows that layer (+68 px) though nothing moves: a "nothing above moves" sweep compares left, top and width, not height (`.crew/sprint/66/placement.md`, `.crew/sprint/66/`).
- The record's list (`.record dl`) is its own query container (`container: record-list / inline-size`), its terms' column `minmax(5.5rem, max-content)` (88 px at 16), and under 15rem each term stands over its fact: a large font on a phone, or the record's 360 px column from 64rem; the pull request's link wraps only where even that can't hold it (36 px at 320). The container is the list, not `.record`, whose steps and hint are absolutely placed in main from 20rem to 48rem: Chromium 153 and 156 apply no layout containment for `container-type` (an absolute child still finds main, overflow still scrolls the page, no stacking context, tested on a bare page), but earlier drafts of the spec did, so an older browser may, and that would make the record their containing block. A list can't change its own gap from its own query, so a term's negative margin gives the row gap back. /crew's `.crew-facts` takes the same pattern under 17rem (`.crew/sprint/70/`).
- Below 1024 a record's h2 sits under the whole header (drawings, captions, count line), so its place moves with data, not code: from sprint 76's row it opened at the fold at 390 x 844, and a chapter line (+35 px), the newest step's hand-off (+44) or a four-line goal ran 13 of 75 under it (B5.7 reads only the tenth row). On a record the Log-wide lines figure now follows the record below 1024 (`.log-open .log-lines`, order 9, Every revision 10), 24 px under the box without its own rule, which doubled the box's edge (Iris, fix round 2): tops 620 to 688, bottoms at most 774. Measure every record from its row after any change above the record (`.crew/sprint/76/`).
- On a record below 1024 and under 60rem tall (every phone in a browser, short tablet windows) the record follows the yard: the ghost's caption and the count line take order 9 after the record, ahead of the lines figure, which takes its 1 px rule and 16 px back; the question or chapter line, then the record, stand first under the ghost (14 px from 768; below 768 `--yard-band`, the 62 px plus `--hint-line` the step row and hint take, one custom property for all three margins); the drawings cap at `clamp(46rem, (100svh - 28rem) * 2.5, 100%)` from 768 to 1023; the goal is `--step-xl` below 22.5rem; `.record-rev` line-height 1 below 768. 60rem tall and up is sprint 76's page, and so is 1024 up and /log (0 boxes moved): any line added above a short window's record must fit 5 px at 360 x 640 (/log/4) and 30 at 900 x 700 (the newest) (`.crew/sprint/77/placement.md`, `.crew/sprint/77/decisions.md`).
